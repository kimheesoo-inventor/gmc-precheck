Why check before you request a review

If review requests keep failing, Google can make you wait before you can ask again, and that wait can get longer after each failed request (Google's help page on reviews). So it is worth fixing everything you can find before you ask.

What I check

Based on Google's Misrepresentation policy and its guide for Shopify's Google & YouTube app:

  • Contact details: a business name, address, email and phone shown on the site, and the same everywhere, with no sample email or phone left over from the theme.
  • Policies: refund, shipping and privacy pages linked from the site, with no template blanks left in them.
  • Returns: return window, conditions and who pays return shipping are stated.
  • Shipping and price: shipping costs and delivery times are shown before checkout, and the total price is clear.
  • Business location: where you operate and ship from is not hidden or unclear.

Examples found on real Shopify stores

  • Template text still in a policy, such as [INSERT RETURN ADDRESS] or [LINK TO REFUND POLICY].
  • A theme's sample contact details, such as support@example.com or +1 (123) 456-7890.
  • Footer social icons that still point to Shopify's own social accounts.
  • A business name or address that differs from one page to another.
  • A mistyped email ending such as .ccom.

These are examples seen on public store pages. They are not a list of what Google flagged in any particular case, and not a measure of how common each one is.

Prefer to check it yourself first? Use the free store-page checklist for Shopify, which shows where to fix each item in Shopify admin. You can also read what 119 public Merchant Center Community threads show.

What you get

  1. A list of the issues found on your public pages, each with the page it is on.
  2. Suggested text you can paste in. Anything only you can know, like your return address, is marked for you to fill in.
  3. Draft notes for your review request that list only what was actually fixed.

The pre-check says which pages were read. It does not look inside your Merchant Center or Shopify admin, so it cannot see everything Google sees. For example, identity verification and whether your product data matches your site are outside it, and Google may ask you to complete them before a review.
