# gmc-precheck

Source of a one-page site: a free written pre-check for Shopify stores that are suspended or warned in Google Merchant Center for Misrepresentation.

- Live page: https://kimheesoo-inventor.github.io/gmc-precheck/
- Self-check guide (no request needed): https://kimheesoo-inventor.github.io/gmc-precheck/shopify-misrepresentation-checklist/
- Run by Heesoo Kim, an independent operator in South Korea. Pre-checks and this page are prepared with an AI assistant and checked against Google's published help pages.
- The pre-check reads only public store pages. It does not log in to Merchant Center or Shopify, and it cannot promise reinstatement or a timeline. Google makes the final decision.
- Requests come through a Google Form with explicit consent. Details are used only to reply to the request, are not added to a mailing list, shared or sold, and are deleted on request.
- Visits are counted with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which does not track visitors across other sites.
- Not affiliated with Google or Shopify. Google Merchant Center and Shopify are trademarks of their owners.
